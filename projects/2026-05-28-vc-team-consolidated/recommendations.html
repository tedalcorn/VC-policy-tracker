Consolidated VC team policy recommendations

20 recommendations across 8 institutional authors, consolidated into 11 themes — 6 with multiple supporting articles, 5 singletons.

Themes with multiple articles

5Federal court should appoint a receiver / remediation manager with broad independent powers over Rikers Island.
(Apr 27, 2023, Glazer: “What You Don’t Know Will Hurt You”, Jun 2, 2023, Glazer: “Dying in Darkness on Rikers Island”, Jun 12, 2023, Glazer: “The Rule of Law Applies on Rikers, Too”, Nov 20, 2023, Glazer: “Fixing New York City's Jails: A Federal Receiver? (Closing Remarks)”, May 15, 2025, Glazer: “A Rare Remedy to Rikers’ Persistent Brutality”)
2Close Rikers Island by reducing the jail population to a target floor (~2,200–3,300), with jail used only as a last resort.
(Sep 15, 2021, Glazer: “What to Do About Closing Rikers”, Mar 12, 2024, Glazer: “Pick a Number: The Facts and Politics Behind New York City Limiting the Jail Population to 3,300 People”)
2Fundamentally restructure NYC jail management — overriding union obstacles, prioritizing violence-reduction and rehabilitation modeled on humane systems abroad.
(Jul 26, 2022, Glazer: “The Fatal Cost of Waiting: What Must Happen Now in the City’s Jails”, Mar 12, 2024, Glazer: “Could Our Jails Be a Civic Asset?”)
2Police should focus on solving serious crime — not running community/social-services programs or chasing quality-of-life enforcement.
(Oct 25, 2022, Glazer: “When Police Become Our Government”, Nov 26, 2024, Hall: “The False Promise of Police Crackdowns in New York City”)
2On subways and public transit, respond with specialized behavioral-health crisis teams and proven policing/social-service strategies — not National Guard or reactive enforcement.
(Mar 30, 2023, del Pozo: “Daniel Penny, Jordan Neely and All of Us In Between”, Mar 7, 2024, del Pozo: “Surging the Guard Won’t Safeguard the Subways”)
2The next NYPD commissioner — paired with a competent Deputy Mayor for Public Safety — must restore governance standards, organizational integrity, and a sustained crime-fighting strategy.
(Oct 10, 2024, Glazer: “What Kind of NYPD Commissioner Do We Need?”, Dec 5, 2024, Glazer: “Police Commissioner Jessica Tisch vs. New York City Crime — and Corruption”)

Standalone recommendations

Mayor Mamdani should have called for more decisive city response to the snowball incident and acknowledged that a crime was committed, rather than dismissing it.
(Feb 28, 2026, del Pozo: “Of Molehills, Mountains and Snowballs”)
Municipal police departments should demonstrate legitimate policing practices that prioritize restraint and professional judgment over reckless force.
(Jan 27, 2026, del Pozo: “What ICE’s Recklessness Teaches Us About Real Policing”)
Reform New York City's municipal election rules to make competitive general elections the norm rather than the exception.
(Apr 25, 2025, Greenman: “A Real New York Mayoral Election”)
Police departments should carry and administer naloxone to reverse opioid overdoses, leveraging their rapid response capabilities especially in underserved areas.
(Dec 13, 2023, del Pozo: “The Right Role for Police”)
Broken windows policing should be reformed rather than abandoned, though the specific reforms are not detailed in this excerpt.
(Oct 25, 2022, Berman: “Mend It, Don't End It: Salvaging Broken Windows”)
